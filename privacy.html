Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-17

1. No Data Collection

SignaDrop is a fully client‑side web application. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information, PDF files, or signature images on any server. All processing happens inside your browser. Once you close the tab, nothing remains.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage solely to remember your dark/light mode preference. No cookies are set for tracking or advertising purposes. Ad networks (e.g., Adsterra) may place their own cookies; please refer to their privacy policies.

3. Third‑Party Services

We may display advertisements through Adsterra. These advertisers may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits. SignaDrop does not have access to or control over these cookies.

4. Data Security

Since no data is transmitted to our infrastructure, your documents remain as secure as your own device. We recommend keeping your browser and operating system up to date.

5. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact

If you have any questions, please reach out via our GitHub repository or social media channels (coming soon).
