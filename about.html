About SignaDrop

SignaDrop was born from a simple need: sign documents without printing, scanning, or uploading sensitive files to a server.

We believe your documents are yours alone. That's why SignaDrop processes everything directly inside your browser. No data is ever sent to our servers – because there are no servers. Your PDF, your signature, your privacy.

How It Works

  • 📤 Upload a PDF (max 10 MB)
  • 🖊️ Draw your signature with your finger, stylus, or mouse on the built‑in pad
  • 🖱️ Drag the signature image to the exact line in your document
  • ⬇️ Download the signed PDF – ready to share

Our Technology

SignaDrop uses open‑source libraries pdf.js and pdf‑lib to render and modify PDFs securely on the client side. The entire tool is a static website, meaning it loads fast, works offline, and respects your bandwidth.

Why "SignaDrop"?

The name combines signature and drop – because that's exactly what you do: you drop your signature onto the page. Simple, intuitive, and a little playful.
